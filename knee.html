The whole picture matters

Your knee is part of a bigger picture.

Knee pain can have different causes. At Karmanya, your doctor considers the knee itself alongside digestion, daily habits and your wider health before discussing authentic Kerala Ayurveda therapies.

Topics your doctor may review

  • Digestion & gut health

    Appetite, digestion, bowel habits and food tolerance are part of your Ayurvedic assessment.

  • Metabolic health

    Your doctor reviews relevant weight, blood-sugar and medical history when planning care.

  • Everyday habits & exposure

    Sleep, smoking, alcohol, work routines and environmental exposures help build the full picture.

  • Nerve & movement function

    Strength, sensation and movement patterns are reviewed alongside your symptoms.

  • Oxidative stress & tissue health

    Oxidative stress is a research topic in tissue health. It is not assumed to be the cause of your pain.

These topics help guide a discussion. They do not establish a diagnosis or prove that any one factor caused your pain.

Do I need an X-ray or MRI?

An X-ray is not required for every person with knee pain, and routine repeat films are not a measure of treatment success for everyone. Your doctor will explain whether imaging could help guide care. If advised, you can use a reliable independent diagnostic centre and bring the report. Progress also includes symptoms and everyday function.

Is this right for your knee?

An assessment helps establish whether this care is suitable for you.

Topics to discuss at your consultation

  • Knee pain or stiffness that has lasted for months, such as osteoarthritis or cartilage wear
  • Pain on stairs, when squatting, or getting up from the floor
  • Been advised surgery and want to explore conservative care first
  • Found that painkillers or physiotherapy help only for a while

See a doctor or hospital first if you have

  • A fresh injury or fall, or a knee that can't take your weight
  • A knee that suddenly locks or gives way
  • Fever with a hot, red, swollen knee: get medical care the same day

Questions people ask before booking

What does it cost?

The in-clinic assessment is ₹500 for about 45 minutes. Any proposed therapy or medicine costs are explained separately before you decide to begin.

How is my appointment confirmed?

Send your request and visit preference. The clinic calls during opening hours, 10 AM to 8 PM, to confirm availability and agree an appointment time. No payment is taken online; the ₹500 consultation fee is paid at the clinic.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Bring any existing X-ray or MRI reports of your knee, a list of your medicines and comfortable clothing. New imaging is considered only when clinically useful.

Is this an online consultation?

This page is for in-clinic assessments at Karmanya Ayurveda, Pimple Saudagar, Pune. Call the clinic if you have questions before visiting.

How many sessions will I need?

Your doctor explains the proposed duration and review intervals after assessment. There is no single course or recovery time that suits everyone; the plan is reviewed according to your response.

What happens during Janu Basti?

When prescribed, a ring of dough holds warm medicated oil over the knee while a therapist monitors your comfort. Tell the therapist if the heat or procedure feels uncomfortable.

Can I keep taking my current medicines?

Bring a list of all medicines and supplements you take. Your doctor reviews the plan in that context. Do not stop or change prescribed medicines on your own.

Can Ayurveda replace surgery?

A consultation can explore suitable care options, but Ayurveda cannot be promised to remove the need for surgery. If surgery has been advised, discuss that recommendation with your treating doctor or relevant specialist before changing the plan.

How will my progress be reviewed?

Your doctor reviews symptoms and everyday function against your starting point and discusses whether care should continue, change or stop. Any repeat imaging is guided by clinical need.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Booking ahead helps the clinic confirm availability. Call or submit a consultation request; your appointment is confirmed when the clinic agrees a time with you.
