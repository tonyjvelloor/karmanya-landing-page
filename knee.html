How the knee treatment works

The aim is to ease pain and stiffness, nourish the joint and get you moving comfortably, using therapies practised in Kerala for generations.

Janu Basti
Warm medicated oil is held over the knee inside a ring of herbal dough, giving the joint deep, sustained warmth.
Patra Pinda Sweda
Warm poultices of medicinal leaves are pressed around the joint to help ease swelling and stiffness.
Lepam
A herbal paste applied over the knee to calm inflammation.
Medicines and daily guidance
Classical formulations from Arya Vaidya Sala Kottakkal and Vaidyaratnam, with diet and movement advice to help you keep the gains.

Most knee plans run 7 to 14 sessions, with a review every two weeks. Your doctor sets the exact course after examining you.

Is this right for your knee?

We would rather tell you now than waste your time.

Likely a good fit if you have

  • Knee pain or stiffness that has lasted for months, such as osteoarthritis or cartilage wear
  • Pain on stairs, when squatting, or getting up from the floor
  • Been advised surgery and want to explore conservative care first
  • Found that painkillers or physiotherapy help only for a while

See a doctor or hospital first if you have

  • A fresh injury or fall, or a knee that can't take your weight
  • A knee that suddenly locks or gives way
  • Fever with a hot, red, swollen knee: get medical care the same day

Questions people ask before booking

Can this help me avoid knee replacement?

For many people with early to moderate knee arthritis, conservative care can reduce pain and improve movement enough to delay or avoid surgery. It isn't right for everyone. After examining you and your reports, the doctor will tell you honestly whether Ayurvedic care is worth trying, or whether you should see an orthopaedic surgeon.

How many sessions will I need?

The doctor decides after your assessment. For knee conditions, 7 to 14 consecutive sessions are typical, with a review every two weeks.

Is Janu Basti painful?

No. Warm medicated oil is held over the knee inside a ring of herbal dough. Most people find it relaxing.

What does it cost?

The assessment is ₹500, paid at the clinic. The cost of therapy depends on the plan the doctor prescribes, and you'll know it before you start.

Can I keep taking my current medicines?

In most cases, yes. Bring a list of everything you take. Please don't stop any medicine on your own; the doctor will guide you on any changes.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any X-ray or MRI reports of your knee, a list of your medicines, and loose trousers that roll up above the knee.

Is this an online consultation?

No. Everything happens in person at the clinic, because the doctor needs to examine your knee and take your pulse for Nadi Pariksha.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are seen when there is space, but booked patients get priority and wait less. Booking takes a minute on WhatsApp or with the form above.
